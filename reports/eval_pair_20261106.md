# Two new people: leave-two-out evaluation, 06 November 2026

*A record of that day, left as it was. Since then the files dsp.py, features.py and gait.py became chain.py and walks.py, `ui.always_choose` was removed (the app always names someone), and `eval-pair` prints only unless `--write`.*

**Chosen: M1 learned whitening on the 'core' features (15), calibration C3 interleaved fresh enrolment (K = 3). Condition 1: 92.0 %, condition 2: 75.9 % (speed alone 71.8 % and 55.1 %: a lead of +20.2 and +20.8 points). It beat M0 (84.1 % and 64.5 %).**

**Selection rule.** Fixed before running. The candidate with the highest mean on condition 1 (K = 3, no calibration) wins. It replaces M0 only if it beats M0 by at least 3 points on condition 1 AND does not lose to M0 on condition 2. If two candidates are within 1 point, the simpler one is taken (M0 < M1 < M2). A calibration is adopted on top of the winner only if it raises the mean of conditions 2 and 3 by at least 1 point without costing more than 1 point on condition 1; for K, the smallest K within 1 point of the best. tau is re-fitted from the leave-two-out decisions so that at most about 15 % of condition-1 walks are not sure.

Data: Room B identification walks, RX2 only (0.5 m beside the TX), the six participants P1-P6; 418 walks the chain saw (209 towards the boards, S). Tests are S walks only. Every walk went through the app's own causal chain (dsp.py, walks.py, features.py, gait.py), as live. For each of the 15 pairs, every learned part (standardisation, whitening, pairwise model, prior_sd, tau) was fitted on the other four people only; each person played Uzayr once and the visitor once, 50 random draws each. Numbers are mean ± SD over the 15 pairs [95 % interval of the mean over pairs; pairs share people, so it is approximate]. Chance is 50 %. CPU 56 s.

Conditions: **c1** within a recording (day-1 natural); **c2** across recordings, same day (day-2 natural -> slow/fast); **c3** stale Uzayr (his enrolment from day 2, tests on day 1); **c4** across days (day 1 -> day 2).

**Honesty notes.** (1) The 'core' set was added after a 10-draw dry run showed the stride-cycle features are missing in about 4 walks in 10 (the step rate is found in 61 % of S walks with one link), so the grid of 7 candidates was fixed after one look at the data; choosing the best of them on condition 1 is mildly optimistic. (2) Condition 1 still has each person's walks in one recording block, so a feature can tell recordings apart; condition 2 breaks that. With interleaved enrolment (C3) both people are recorded in the same minutes, which removes the recording difference the block confound feeds on: expect the live number between conditions 2 and 1. (3) M3 (an embedding network) was not built: six people is very few, and M1 already passed the bar.
